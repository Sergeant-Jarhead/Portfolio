About me

that’s me!

I’m Jared, a designer based in Singapore, and I’m a lot of “-ers”. A listener and an observer first: I work closely with users to pick up the behaviours and needs they don’t say out loud. Then a communicator, an iterator and a tinkerer, testing ideas early and often until something unexpected turns up. It’s a simple way of working, but it’s how I naturally get along with people, and it lands on solutions that solve the client’s real problem without compromise.
